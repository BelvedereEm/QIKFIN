# QIKFIN

**Quick Finance Webpage App**

A simple, install-anywhere personal finance tracker — balances, budgets, and projections at a glance, with real morning push notifications on iOS.

</div>

---

## ✨ Features

- 💰 **Current Balance** & **Available Balance**
- 🏦 **Savings / Checking** tracking
- 📅 **Left to Spend** until your next paycheck
- 📈 **Projections** — see where your money's headed
- 🗂 **Budget / Categories**
- 🔔 **iOS Push Notifications** — a real morning update at **4:30 AM EST**, even when your phone is locked
- 🔒 **Firestore Protected** with **App Check**

---

## 📲 Install on iPhone

1. Open QikFin in **Safari**
2. Tap **Share** → **Add to Home Screen**
3. Make sure **"Use as Web App"** is selected
4. Open QikFin from the new home screen icon and **allow notifications** when prompted

That's it — you'll get a notification each morning with anything due today or tomorrow.
